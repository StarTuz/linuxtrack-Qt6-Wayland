ONNX Model Files
================

The head-localizer.onnx and head-pose-*.onnx model files are trained and
provided by the OpenTrack project under the same ISC license as above.

These models are designed for real-time head pose estimation from webcam
images and output:
- Head position (x, y) and size in image coordinates
- Head rotation as a quaternion

Model variants:
- head-localizer.onnx: Face detection/localization (288x224 input)
- head-pose-0.4-big-int8.onnx: Quantized pose estimator (recommended)
- head-pose-0.4-small-f32.onnx: Lightweight float32 variant
